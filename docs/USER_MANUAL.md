# 📖 DineFlow AI - End-to-End User Manual & Operations Guide

## 1. Customer Digital QR Ordering Guide

### 1.1 Accessing the Menu
1. Scan the Table Tent QR code placed on your dining table with any smartphone camera or Google Lens.
2. The browser immediately loads the digital menu pre-configured for your table (e.g., `Table #3`).

### 1.2 Browsing Dishes & Using Smart Filters
- **Dietary Switch**: Tap **`🟢 Veg`** or **`🔴 Non-Veg`** to filter items based on dietary preferences.
- **Category Bar**: Tap on `🍲 Mains`, `🥟 Starters`, `🍞 Breads`, `🥤 Beverages`, or `🍨 Desserts` to jump to a specific section.
- **Voice Ordering**: Tap the **`🎙️`** microphone button and speak in **English, Hindi, or Gujarati** (e.g., *"બે પનીર બટર મસાલા અને ત્રણ નાન"*). DineFlow AI automatically adds items to your basket.

### 1.3 Placing Orders & Live Tracking
1. Tap on the bottom floating **`🛒 Cart`** button.
2. Choose your cooking preferences (e.g., *🌶️ Extra Spicy*, *🧪 Jain Food*).
3. Select your Payment Method:
   - **`💵 Pay at Counter`**: Pay cash/card at the billing counter after your meal.
   - **`📱 Instant UPI QR`**: Scan the dynamic on-screen UPI QR code with GPay/PhonePe/Paytm for instant digital payment.
4. Tap **`🚀 Confirm & Place Order`**. The ticket is dispatched to the Kitchen KDS in real-time.
5. The **Live Order Tracker** displays real-time progress steps (*Received -> Preparing in Kitchen -> Ready to Serve*).

---

## 2. Cashier POS Terminal Guide

### 2.1 Table Management & Billing
- **Table Selector**: Click on any table card to view open bills, accumulated orders, or occupied status.
- **Merging Orders**: If guests place additional orders from their phone, they automatically append to the active table session.
- **Print Hub**: Click **`🖨️ Print Bill`** to trigger ESC/POS 58mm/80mm thermal receipts or KOT kitchen slips.
- **Z-Report & Shift Close**: Access the end-of-day summary report to reconcile gross sales, cash drawers, and digital UPI collections.

---

## 3. Kitchen Display System (KDS) Guide

- **Live Ticket Cards**: Display table numbers, ordered item names, quantities, and custom cooking instructions.
- **Priority Timers**: Visual color indicators notify chefs of ticket turnaround time.
- **Start Cooking / Mark Ready**: Tap **`Start Cooking`** to notify waiters, and **`Mark Ready`** when the dish is ready for pickup.
- **Audible Alerts**: Automatic chime alerts play hands-free whenever a new order arrives.
